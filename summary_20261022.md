# Overnight summary, 21-22 October 2026

As of 01:20 on 2026-10-22. Runs still in progress; the analysis below is complete and final.

## One-paragraph version

The night's computations filled the last two gaps in the TYK2 MSA-depth ladder and confirmed it
saturates. The night's *analysis* did something larger: every structure the campaign has ever
produced was re-scored by superposition against experimental references instead of by domain-centroid
distance, at per-seed resolution. Three things came out of that. **The variant effect is real and
large** - V678F takes wild type from 8 % to 75 % canonical open at 64/256, and V678A and V678I also
survive correction. **The closed state is template-induced** - 2 of 276 template-free structures
reach it, against 47 of 47 when 4OLI is supplied. And **the pipeline's own confidence signal is blind
to the arrangement** - pLDDT is identical across structures that match a cryo-EM reference and
structures 40 A from anything known, so the pLDDT-based objective at the heart of the method
contributes nothing to the conformational result. Separately, a labelling error was found and fixed:
the two cryo-EM references are mouse **JAK1**, not JAK2, which means there is no experimental
multi-domain structure for TYK2, JAK2 or JAK3 at all.

**The same variants hold the closed state perfectly when given a template.** The release assay is
31/31 closed across WT, V678F, V678M and V678I, plus 16/16 for template-throughout runs - 47

and holds the closed state completely from 4OLI. That contrast, now measured at full power on both
sides, is the cleanest statement the TYK2 work supports.

## Three things that are not what we thought

**1. The closed state is template-induced, not predicted.** 2 of 276 template-free structures land
within 15 A of 4OLI or AF2, against 47 of 47 template-assisted ones. The "compact < 60 A" bin
contains **zero** closed structures in free runs - so "WT 7/16 compact at 64/256", the figure that
made 64/256 the campaign's informative depth, was never counting closed structures.

**2. pLDDT cannot see the arrangement.** Median pLDDT is 80.2 for closed, 80.4 for canonical open,
80.1 for structures more than 15 A from everything - interquartile width 0.7 points across 323
structures spanning 39-95 A. Within each MSA depth the correlation between pLDDT and distance-to-
nearest-reference is absent and the signs disagree (+0.02, +0.14, -0.28, +0.06). The pooled
rho = -0.47 is Simpson's paradox: depth drives both. Consequence: the objective
J = (1 - pLDDT_norm) + 0.1 x violation_loss selects among seeds at fixed depth, exactly where pLDDT
is uninformative about state. The honest description of the method is "MSA subsampling plus
external-reference classification", not "guided recycling".

**3. The DMS correlation depends on which dose you read.** The arrangement-based score correlates
with nothing measured (|rho| < 0.09 against abundance and against signalling at 1 U and 100 U). The
*compact fraction* - a different score, on the same structures - gives rho = +0.56 against abundance
(p = 0.12) and **rho = +0.67 against signalling at 100 U (p = 0.050)**, which the earlier work never
tested. At 1 U the sign is the other way. Which dose the scan is read at decides whether the pipeline
looks like an abundance sensor or an activation sensor. One uncorrected test out of six, so: a
hypothesis for the next experiment, not a result.

## Corrections made

- **7T6F and 8EWY are mouse JAK1**, not JAK2: 98.0 % identity to JAK1_MOUSE against 43.1 % to JAK2,
  by alignment. I had asserted mouse JAK2 earlier in the day, citing P52332 - which *is* JAK1_MOUSE.
  The consequence is substantive: both are the same JAK1 construct, so **there is no experimental
  multi-domain structure for TYK2, JAK2 or JAK3 at all**, and every closed-state TYK2 number in the
  project is a comparison against a prediction or a cross-paralogue JAK1 structure.
- Four analysis scripts had used **JAK2 domain windows on a JAK1 chain** as a result. All
  recomputed; conclusions held, digits moved by up to 1.8 A.
- **JAK3's extended state matches cryo-EM open JAK1 at 4.9-7.6 A** - an experimental match, not just
  model-to-model. It still never reaches the active dimer (closest 46.0 A). Its compact bin splits
  into three populations, not one.
- I wrongly claimed per-seed structures were never pushed to the repository, after listing a run
  folder's top level without opening `all/`. They were always there: 15 recycles x 8 seeds per run,
  gzipped, plus an index CSV. The pipeline change I queued on that basis is withdrawn - and the data
  made the full-power variant result above possible.
- Section 41's V678I and V678G counts were inflated by byte-identical re-pushed runs (verified on all
  120 index rows including pLDDT). Corrected above. Same-seed *independent* draws were kept - a seed
  number is not a reproducibility handle under stochastic dropout.

## Infrastructure

The overnight stall had a single cause. `configure()` wrote `TEMPLATE_RECYCLES` only for release
experiments, so once a release run had been ported onto a sandbox the notebook cell kept
`TEMPLATE_RECYCLES = 3` forever. A later template-free run then reported 3, the controller's
`matches()` rejected **its own finished result**, and it aborted after three attempts - while the
inference had completed and written every PDB. It presented exactly as an inference failure. Both
controllers now write that key for every experiment. Each aborted attempt had also minted a new
`RUN_DIR` and re-pushed the same inference, which is why the repository holds 200 run folders for 138
distinct run-level computations.

Also fixed: the missing `stereo_chemical_props.txt` on slot A's replacement sandbox.
