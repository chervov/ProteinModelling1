# Human TYK2 variants implicated in disease

Evidence reviewed 1 November 2026. Human **TYK2** (Gene ID 7297, UniProt P29597). Small variants use NM_003331.5 where available. The repository folder and filename follow the requested JAK3 destination.

## Inherited TYK2 deficiency and mycobacterial susceptibility

The classical disorder is autosomal recessive. The genotype and cytokine response matter. A heterozygous carrier of a deficiency allele does not by that fact have immunodeficiency 35. The ClinVar labels below are variant-level aggregates, while the descriptions summarize the particular patients and experiments.

## Search scope and reproducibility

- **ClinVar.** Retrieved all 1,183 unique Variation IDs returned by `TYK2[gene]` using NCBI ESearch and ESummary. Kept exact germline aggregates Pathogenic, Likely pathogenic and Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic for single-gene TYK2 records or records explicitly indexed to immunodeficiency 35. Broad chromosomal syndromes were excluded. Uncertain and conflicting assertions appear only when there is relevant independent evidence. [Repeat gene search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/?term=TYK2%5Bgene%5D)
- **OMIM.** Matched TYK2 (*176941) and immunodeficiency 35 (#611521) through NCBI Gene and OMIM-authored ClinVar submissions. Direct retrieval of the OMIM entry was unavailable. OMIM submissions that cite the same patient series are one line of evidence. [OMIM gene](https://omim.org/entry/176941) · [Phenotype](https://omim.org/entry/611521) · [NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/7297) · [Example OMIM submission](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/1703747/)
- **Literature.** Searched TYK2 with deficiency, immunodeficiency 35, mycobacterial disease, tuberculosis, gain of function, immunodysregulation, leukemia and fusion terms. Traced reported substitutions to cohort tables, later functional studies and recent case reports. LOVD and Orphanet supplied cross-checks. Coverage is bounded by publicly retrievable and indexed material. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=TYK2+AND+%28deficiency+OR+mutation+OR+variant+OR+fusion%29) · [LOVD](https://databases.lovd.nl/shared/variants/TYK2/unique) · [Orphanet](https://www.orpha.net/en/disease/gene/TYK2)
- **Cancer data.** Selected mutation profiles ending in `_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018_mutations` and queried each profile with its sequenced sample list and Entrez Gene ID 7297. Counts use the returned protein-change strings. Fusion evidence comes separately from tumor sequencing papers. This survey covers the TCGA PanCancer Atlas collection within cBioPortal. [Example API query](https://www.cbioportal.org/api/molecular-profiles/skcm_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018_mutations/mutations?sampleListId=skcm_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018_sequenced&entrezGeneId=7297&projection=DETAILED)
- **Functional screens.** The 2026 TYK2 deep mutational scan measured IFN-α signaling and protein abundance across nearly all single amino-acid substitutions. An abnormal score supplies functional evidence. Selective IL-23 defects require a separate assay, and assigning a disease phenotype also requires human genetic evidence. [PMID 42268925](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42268925/)
