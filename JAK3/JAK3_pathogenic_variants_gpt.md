# Human JAK3 variants implicated in disease

**1 November 2026.** The ClinVar gene search returned **1,439 records**. Of these, **146** met the germline pathogenicity and gene-scope criteria below: **85 pathogenic, 51 likely pathogenic and 10 pathogenic/likely pathogenic**. Seventeen of the 146 were reviewed by an expert panel. Literature tables distinguish inherited JAK3 deficiency, rare germline activation, and acquired cancer mutations. The last table measures recurrence in tumor samples. Sections overlap and should not be summed.

JAK3 (NM_000215.4, UniProt P52333) transmits signals from receptors that share the common γ chain. Biallelic loss of function typically causes severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID) with few or absent T and NK cells and circulating B cells. Residual activity can produce Omenn syndrome or later combined immunodeficiency. Activating substitutions found in malignant clones have a different clinical interpretation. [Founding genetic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7659163/) · [ClinGen SCID specifications](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41104538/) · [T-ALL experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25193870/)

## Sources and coverage

- **ClinVar and ClinGen.** Queried NCBI ESearch with `JAK3[gene]`, retrieved all unique IDs, then read the germline aggregate from ESummary. Included records naming JAK3 in their transcript title or listing JAK3 as the sole gene. Excluded broad chromosomal imbalances. Counts refer to Variation IDs, including one complex allele, and do not imply 146 independently observed affected patients. Of the 1,439 records, six had an explicit somatic oncogenicity aggregate, all uncertain. The literature supplies separate oncogenic evidence. [ClinVar gene query](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/?term=JAK3%5Bgene%5D) · [ClinGen SCID panel specifications](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41104538/)
- **OMIM and rare disease resources.** Cross-checked the gene (*600173) and autosomal recessive SCID phenotype (#600802) against NCBI Gene, Orphanet and legacy ClinVar records. Direct OMIM access was blocked. The historical JAK3base bibliography and the early review of 27 mutations provide a route to pre-HGVS reports. Historical names are retained when a modern mapping was uncertain. [OMIM gene](https://omim.org/entry/600173) · [OMIM phenotype](https://omim.org/entry/600802) · [NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/3718/) · [Orphanet](https://www.orpha.net/en/disease/gene/JAK3) · [JAK3base](https://structure.bmc.lu.se/idbase/JAK3base/index.php?content=pubs%2FIDbases) · [Historical mutation series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11668610/) · [LOVD](https://databases.lovd.nl/shared/view/JAK3)
- **Literature.** Searched JAK3 with SCID, deficiency, hypomorphic, Omenn, reversion, splice, gain of function, lymphoproliferation, dermatitis, leukemia, lymphoma and fusion, followed by allele-specific searches. Included selected conference abstracts explicitly marked as such. The literature tables prioritize variants with identifiable human genotypes or a directly relevant functional experiment. They are not a complete export of every tumor sequencing supplement. [PubMed search](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=JAK3+AND+%28SCID+OR+deficiency+OR+mutation+OR+variant+OR+fusion%29) · [Large pediatric T-ALL cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39143224/)
- **cBioPortal.** Selected all public mutation profiles with study IDs ending in `_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018`, requested their sequenced sample lists for Entrez Gene ID 3718, and grouped the returned protein-change labels. Cohort links identify the contributing studies. No inference of germline origin or oncogenicity was made from recurrence alone.
