## New: JAK3 M592F destabilises the compact arrangement (MSA 128/512)

First JAK3 runs of any kind. OpenFold model_1, guided recycling, no template, 15 recycles, 16 seeds, paired
(the same seeds in both). M592 is the JAK3 position equivalent to JAK2 V617 and JAK1 V658.

| | final FERM–JH1 (Å), 16 seeds | max | > 70 Å |
|---|---|--:|--:|
| **JAK3 wild type** | 42 42 42 42 43 43 43 50 51 54 54 54 55 55 56 56 | 56 | **0/16** |
| **JAK3 M592F** | 44 48 50 52 53 53 54 55 55 56 58 **80 94 94 95 97** | 97 | **5/16** |

Fisher exact, > 70 Å: **p = 0.043**. Mean FERM–JH1 across recycles 2–15: wild type ~49–51 Å, M592F ~65 Å, a
consistent ~15 Å shift rather than a tail artefact.

Runs: [JAK3/runs/JAK3_20261016_125830](JAK3/runs/JAK3_20261016_125830/conclusions.md) (131 min) and
[JAK3/runs/JAK3_M592F_20261016_105409](JAK3/runs/JAK3_M592F_20261016_105409/conclusions.md) (135 min).

**Reading it.** JAK3 wild type at this depth is uniformly compact and never exceeds 56 Å. M592F keeps 11 of 16 seeds
compact but sends 5 into an extended arrangement the wild type never reaches. The direction matches **JAK1 V658F**
(which likewise removed the compact/closed states at 128/512) and is **opposite to JAK2 V617F** at the same depth
(which went detached → open). So the equivalent mutation does not act in one consistent direction across the family.

**Caveats, which matter here.**
- The p-value depends on the threshold: 0.043 at > 70 Å but 0.10 at > 80 Å. Treat it as suggestive, not established.
- **Only one reference structure was available for JAK3** (AF2 AF-P52333-F1, itself compact at 41.5 Å), so every
  conformation was "nearest AF2" by default and we cannot yet say *what* the extended state is — open, 7T6F-like, or
  other. Offline classification against ma-l9b1f, 7T6F and 4OLI mapped onto JAK3 numbering is the next step and needs
  no GPU.
- Unlike JAK1 and JAK2, the **AlphaFold model of JAK3 is compact (41.5 Å), not open (~93 Å)**, so the open/closed
  thresholds carried over from JAK1/JAK2 do not transfer directly.

Still running on molab: JAK3 wild type at 512/8192, 96/384 and a closed-template release; mutants A573V, M511I, R657Q
at 128/512.

## New: TYK2 campaign opened

TYK2 is the only JAK with an **experimental closed structure** — 4OLI (JH2–JH1, autoinhibited, 2.8 Å, Lupardus et al.
2014). For JAK1/JAK2/JAK3 the closed reference is a model, so TYK2 is the one place the method can be checked against
ground truth.

**Interface analysis of 4OLI** (solvent accessibility, which residues are buried by closing):

- The JH2–JH1 interface buries **2261 Å²** across 36 residues.
- **P982 (JH1)** is 47 % exposed on isolated JH1 and buries **59.8 Å²** on closing — rank 9 of 520 residues. It makes
  28 cross-domain contacts, of which **12 come from the CG and CD atoms that proline→alanine deletes**. Its JH2
  partners are S663, Q664, V665, S666. Proline is conserved here in all four JAKs (JAK1 P960, JAK2 P933, JAK3 P906).
  Prediction: **P982A destabilises the closed state specifically**, since those atoms are solvent-facing when open.
- **V678 (JH2)**, the position equivalent to JAK2 V617 / JAK1 V658, makes **zero** contacts with JH1 — any effect is
  allosteric. Note F672, six residues away, is the single largest interface residue (86.9 Å²).
- Of the other variants examined, **none is at the interface**: TYK2 H667 (= JAK3 Y581), V639 (= JAK2 V578),
  L866 (= JAK2 L805) and A1156 (= JAK2 V1110) all bury **0.0 Å²** on closing.

**Orianne et al. comparison reproduced.** Their Fig. 1D reports 0.893 Å over 318 pruned atoms between their closed
JAK1 model and 4OLI; we get 0.93 Å over 337. But that pruned measure does **not** discriminate: their *open* model
scores 0.84 Å by the same method. Superposing JH2 and measuring where JH1 lands gives 4.2 Å for the closed model and
80.6 Å for the open one — so their conclusion holds, by a different measure than the one quoted.

**Running on Colab A100:** TYK2 wild type and TYK2 P982A, 4OLI as template in recycles 1–3 then released, MSA 128/512,
15 recycles, seeds 0–15, paired. Readout is JH1 RMSD against the mapped 4OLI reference after superposing JH2 — not
FERM–JH1, since 4OLI has no FERM-SH2.

## Infrastructure notes added today

- **molab runs at most 2 notebooks at once**; opening, duplicating or creating a third silently shuts down the oldest
  (HTTP 410). This killed a JAK3 wild-type run mid-inference. Check the Running count before opening anything.
- The Colab notebook needed three fixes before TYK2 could run: the ColabFold MSA calls had **no retry** (a transient
  timeout killed two runs ten minutes in), the notebook did **not support `pdb:` template sources** (only AF2 and
  ModelArchive), and `run_result.json` recorded only FERM–JH1, which is meaningless for a 4OLI-templated run. All
  three are fixed in `~/TYK2_campaign/make_tyk2.py`.
