# JAK2 variants across two panels of 20 human non-JAK tyrosine kinases

Search date: 31 October 2026. Start with curated_evidence.tsv for primary papers and named database records. For the complete map and gaps, open variant_map.tsv. For top 20 by either criterion, sort ranking.tsv by JH1_rank or JH2_rank. Inputs include ten variants from the lower half of the figure and fourteen changes from the upper half; the upper group includes the corrected two-part JAK2 allele S755R/R938Q in cis (PMID 24398328).

## Biological findings

* V578I maps to FGFR3 V505I and EGFR V742I, in the beta3 ATP-binding lysine region. The FGFR3 mutant failed a direct test of increased growth-factor-independent proliferation in MCF10A cells. Both target variants have conflicting germline ClinVar classifications. This is a negative result for one phenotype in one system.
* L611S projects to the same Asn-to-Ser change in multiple FGFRs, although wild-type JAK2 is leucine. FGFR3 N540S has clinical segregation data and elevated autophosphorylation. FGFR2 N549S has transforming-activity evidence and a somatic cancer cohort record. FGFR1 N546S was found alongside K656E in a glioblastoma. The FGFR molecular brake has direct structural support (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2094128/); the JAK2 regulatory mechanism must be tested separately.
* R938Q maps to RET R813Q with conserved start and endpoint. It impairs RET signaling in cell assays and has been found in Hirschsprung disease. The JAK2 family carried a double allele with S755R. ABL1 L324Q, a different starting residue, was observed in a BCR::ABL1-positive clone before imatinib.
* The Val-to-Phe projection of V617F is retained in TNK2 V196F, NTRK2 V608F and CSK V256F. No direct activating result was verified for any of those three. At the aligned NTRK3 site, G608S retained apoptosis induction in RKO cells. For V617I, ABL1 T306I has a patient observation in a doctoral thesis; distinct starting residue and weaker evidence than a replicated assay.
* Remaining variants and selected same-codon observations, including unresolved FERM positions, are in variant_map.tsv. Germline clinical classifications, tumor observations, compound alleles and functional tests are distinct types of evidence.

## Alignment and ranking

The comparison comprises one KEGG protein sequence per gene for 87 non-JAK human tyrosine kinase/kinase-like candidates, with JAK2 as reference (88 input sequences). KEGG gene links and frozen sequences are provided. This is a sequence-panel ranking, not an all-human-protein or all-isoform ranking. The published KinCore MSA could not be retrieved as machine-readable sequence data in this run; its 497-domain resource was not used to generate these ranks. KinCore background: https://dunbrack.fccc.edu/kincore/alignment . Remote sequence alignment, including in loops, is not a structural superposition.

Each candidate's full sequence was searched independently with JAK2 JH1 residues 849–1124 and JH2 residues 536–812 using Smith–Waterman/BLOSUM62 and gap open 10, extension 0.5. Rank by raw score within each equal-length query. Ties use paired-residue identity and then gene symbol. Percent identity divides identities by paired residues. Coverage divides paired residues by query length. Seven genes overlap between the top 20 lists. For the primary JH1 panel all 24 variants are tabulated. For the 13 additional JH2 panel members the nine JH2 changes are tabulated. The 597 comparisons include gaps and absent domains; they are not 597 observed alleles.

For residue maps a target window extends 14 residues on both sides of the JH1 match. JH1 and JH2 were aligned independently to that window using four local gap settings (10/0.5, 12/0.5, 10/1, 14/1). A motif-constrained alignment maps beta3 lysine, gatekeeper and DFG/DPG aspartate. Target anchor positions were derived from the reference JH1 local alignment and are sequence estimates. JAK2 landmarks: JH1 K882/M929/D994, JH2 K581/Q626/D699. An anchored BLOSUM62 global alignment was performed between consecutive landmarks. FASTAs give the alignment and one-based starting positions; map rows state whether anchored and all four local mappings agree. The source for JH2 structure is https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3414675/ . PTK7 has a pseudokinase fold; a measured biochemical effect in another kinase cannot be assigned to it. N-terminal JAK2 FERM positions lack reliable sequence-level transfer. For SH2, only targets with the relevant architecture were attempted; in tandem-SH2 SYK only the best match was recorded and register remains provisional. Absence of a numerical projection is intentional.

## Evidence search and coverage

Direct record and primary-paper checks are distinguished from secondary indexes in curated_evidence.tsv. query_log.tsv shows the gene/allele queries for accessible numerical projections, including gap-sensitive ones. candidate string matches were inspected and wrong-gene hits excluded; counts in that log count string matches, not findings. Indexed searches alone can miss closed supplements, unindexed calls, other isoforms and alternate HGVS notation. Database_site_hits.tsv lists exact or other alleles at the same target residue from accessible indexed Sugi/ClinVar Miner tables, CanProVar and the Japanese Cancer Genome Atlas. Index entries are leads unless explicitly checked against the primary source. Do not infer an allele from an in-silico proposal; synonymous records are included but clearly marked '='. Database gene-page records may use different transcripts and should be confirmed by accession/cDNA before clinical reuse. coverage.tsv gives retrieval status by resource and gene: zero retrieved lines means unavailable, not no variants. Some source views themselves display only a selection of variants. Reference clinical records may evolve.

For the FGFR1–4 saturation screen (PMID 41361008), the primary paper, its design and data deposition were verified; a complete variant-level numerical score matrix could not be retrieved. Individual scores were not inferred. Data set: https://www.mavedb.org/search?search=urn:mavedb:00001220-a . gnomAD frequency and COSMIC identifiers are supplied only when specifically supported by cited sources. CKB and JCGA evidence is linked where relevant. No raw population- or tumor-callset join was performed.
