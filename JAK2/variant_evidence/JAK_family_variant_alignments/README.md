# Human JAK family: alignment and variant evidence

24 JAK2 changes at 23 positions, using S755R/R938Q in cis from the original family paper. Three independent full-length global pairwise alignments to human JAK2 O60674; these are not a four-way multiple alignment. Primary scoring: BLOSUM62, gap opening 10, extension 0.5. Positions also checked with (12,0.5), (10,1), (14,1). Slash-separated cells show all distinct projections with primary first. No gap denotes a missing residue elsewhere; a cell labelled gap means no aligned target residue for that setting. “Already WT” means no substitution in that paralog. Unchanged coordinates across four scores are not proof of structural equivalence. The target letter is copied from the JAK2 variant, even when the paralog has a different starting residue. Stop variants are projected protein-level consequences, not an assertion about a nucleotide change. Sequence sources: JAK1 P23458, JAK2 O60674 and TYK2 P29597 via dbPTM; JAK3 P52333 via COVPDB. Human residue numbering starts at 1.

## Literature findings

- **JAK2 H380Y** — TYK2 S431G was reported in a B-ALL patient; impaired activity in a biochemical assay. The projected substitution is S431Y. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33260630/
- **JAK2 R426*** — JAK3 R402H occurred in trans with Q1007Ter in suspected SCID and reduced STAT5 signaling. R402H differs from the projected stop. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/genetics/articles/10.3389/fgene.2026.1624523/full
- **JAK2 C427R** — Wild-type JAK3 already carries Arg403. A case report cites JAK3 R403C in SCID (reverse change; secondary citation). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1472957/full
- **JAK2 Y317H** — JAK3 F292V reported in a compound heterozygous SCID patient; different substitution. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30032486/
- **JAK2 L611S** — JAK3 L586S is an exact counterpart recovered in growth-factor-independent murine TS1 and TS2 clones; human numbering. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006497120395586 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11108519/
- **JAK2 V617F** — JAK1 V658F and engineered TYK2 V678F activate signaling. JAK3 M592F did not activate in the original assay. JAK3 M592F + L570F could activate STAT5 with JAK1 present. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16239216/ https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7016850/ https://www.nature.com/articles/nsmb.2673
- **JAK2 V617I** — JAK1 V658I reported in a patient with MPN. No verified JAK3 M592I or TYK2 V678I primary study. https://haematologica.org/article/view/8178
- **JAK2 V625F** — JAK3 V599G reported with W709R in compound heterozygous SCID; different substitution, genotype-level evidence. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30032486/
- **JAK2 R867Q** — JAK3 R840C reported with CTLA4 haploinsufficiency; activating in functional studies. Different substitution. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2017.01824/full
- **JAK2 T875N** — JAK3 T848A recovered in growth-factor-independent murine clones; different substitution. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006497120395586 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11108519/
- **JAK2 R938Q** — TYK2 R987Q appears in a patent describing R987Q and D988N editing in Jurkat cells. No isolated R987Q functional conclusion. JAK2 R938Q originally in cis with S755R. https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2022175674A1/en https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2013-05-504555
- **JAK2 S755R** — Component of JAK2 S755R/R938Q in cis. https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2013-05-504555

Search scope: gene-specific primary papers and indexed supplements for the projected JAK3 and TYK2 substitutions; targeted follow-up on other substitutions at aligned sites. A negative result means no primary report verified here, not proven absence in registries, sequencing callsets or unindexed supplements. The JAK1 column is included for comparison.
