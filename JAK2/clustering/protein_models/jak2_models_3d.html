The open→closed transition moves JH1 and nothing else: FERM–SH2–JH2 is a rigid body in all five structures, and every per-domain RMSD across these models is 0.10–0.66 Å. Rotate the first panel and the ~50 Å swing of the red JH1 domain is the whole story.

3. Coloured by pLDDT

Confidence is high inside domains and collapses in the linkers. For the AlphaFold DB model we also have PAE, which puts the FERM–JH1 block at 21.0 Å against a 31 Å ceiling — the network is confident about each domain and close to uninformative about where JH1 sits.
