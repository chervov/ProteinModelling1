## The finding in one line

OpenFold, given the wild-type JAK1 sequence, a shallow MSA and **no template**, produced a
JH2–JH1 arrangement **4.9 Å** from **7T6F** — the cryo-EM structure of the active JAK1 dimer —
against 47 Å from open and 83 Å from closed.

It is a **positive control, not a discovery**: 7T6F is itself a JAK1 structure, so this shows the
pipeline can reach a measured arrangement from sequence alone, not that JAK1 is special.
