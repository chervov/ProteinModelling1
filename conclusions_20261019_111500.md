# Main results so far (20261019_111500)

Guided-recycling OpenFold (model_1, 15 recycles), MSA depth as the control variable, mutants paired with
wild type on identical seeds. Structures classified by **JH1 RMSD after superposing JH2** (the domain
arrangement) against references mapped onto each target's numbering.

## 1. MSA depth, not sequence, decides the conformation

Every JAK switches between open and closed arrangements as the alignment is thinned, and each does so at
a different depth. A mutation can only be detected at a depth where wild type visits both states.

| | depth where both states appear | behaviour at 128/512 |
|---|---|---|
| JAK2 | 512/8192 (7 closed / 7 open) | mostly detached JH1 |
| JAK1 | 128/512 | bistable |
| JAK3 | 128/512 (7 of 16 seeds closed-like) | bistable |
| TYK2 | **none found** | uniformly open |

JAK3's switch is unusually sharp: 16/16 compact at 128/512, 15/16 open one step deeper at 256/1024.

## 2. JAK3 — five mutations, three distinct behaviours

Wild type at 128/512 retains the closed arrangement in **7/16** seeds.

| variant | closed-like seeds | effect |
|---|--:|---|
| **M592F** (= JAK2 V617F position) | **1/16** (p = 0.037) | closed state lost; 5 seeds reach the open state, 3.3 Å from the JAK1 open model |
| **A572V** (reported activating) | **1/16** (p = 0.037) | closed state lost, replaced by splayed arrangements |
| **M511I** (reported activating) | 7/16 (p = 0.82) | frequency unchanged, but its closed seeds reach 1–2 Å vs wild type's 16 Å |
| **A573V** (neighbour of A572V) | 12/16 | geometry tightened to 2 Å (rank test p = 0.0024) |
| R657Q (reported activating) | running | – |

**The three reported activating alleles do not behave as one class**: A572V removes the closed state,
M511I does not. A573V — the adjacent alanine, and *not* a reported allele — does the opposite of A572V.

## 3. TYK2 — the ground-truth test, and a negative result

TYK2 is the only JAK with an experimental closed structure (4OLI, autoinhibited JH2–JH1, 2.8 Å).

- **Free prediction never reaches the closed state, at any depth** (32/64 to 512/5120; closest approach
  25.4 Å, 3 of 94 seeds below 30 Å) — unlike JAK1, JAK2 and JAK3, which all reach theirs.
- Yet once 4OLI is imposed as a template, the closed state is **perfectly stable** through twelve
  template-free recycles (all 16 seeds within 2 Å). A real but inaccessible basin.
- **V678F**, the reported activating substitution, abolishes wild type's partial approaches to the
  closed arrangement: 90.7–94.8 Å versus wild type's 41.2–92.0 Å, **p = 0.0008** at 64/256.
- **P982A shows no significant effect at any depth** (p = 0.59 / 0.34 / 0.14), so the structural
  prediction below is not supported — though with wild type never occupying the closed state, the assay
  may have no floor to measure against.

## 4. Structural analysis of seven variants of interest

Interface burial computed in each protein's own closed structure (ΔSASA on closing; > 25 Å² = interface):

- **TYK2 P982 is the only one of the seven at a domain interface** — 47 % exposed alone, **59.8 Å² buried
  on closing**, 28 cross-domain contacts of which **12 are deleted by proline→alanine**. Proline is
  conserved at this position in all four JAKs.
- TYK2 V678, JAK3 Y581, JAK2 V578/L805/V1110 and JAK2 G571 all bury **0.0 Å²** — none touches an
  interface. Any effect of V678F is therefore allosteric, which makes its p = 0.0008 result notable.
- **Sequence predictors are unreliable here.** AlphaMissense scores JAK2 V617F (ClinVar Pathogenic) at
  0.33–0.39 "benign", and TYK2 P1104A (ClinVar Benign) at 0.82 "pathogenic" — wrong in both directions,
  because they are trained on loss of function rather than on conformational gain of function.
- None of the seven variants has a dedicated functional study in the literature.

## 5. Methodological findings

- **Two metrics are needed.** FERM–JH1 detects M592F (p = 0.012) but misses A572V (p = 0.13); the
  arrangement metric does the reverse. Reporting either alone misrepresents at least one variant.
- **The template-release assay does not work** as configured — the template grip is total, leaving no
  dynamic range to detect a mutant.
- **Watch seed diversity.** At TYK2 128/512 all seeds fall within 0.6 Å, and a rank test then returns
  p = 0.011 for a 0.5 Å median difference — significance manufactured from absent variance.
- A numbering error (A573V run in place of the reported A572V) was found and corrected; both were then
  run, and they turned out to have opposite effects.
