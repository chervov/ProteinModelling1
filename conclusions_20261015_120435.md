# When do we get the open and when the closed form? — JAK1 / JAK2 OpenFold guided recycling (20261015_120435)

The domain arrangement depends on two things: whether there's a template, and how deep the MSA is.
Open/closed: JH1 position after superposing JH2 vs the references, and FERM–JH1 centroid distance (open ~92–94 Å, closed ~40–42 Å; see [open_close.md](open_close.md)).

## When we get open
- With an open template (the AF2 model).
- With no template and a deep MSA. This is AlphaFold's default answer: confident, the same in every seed, settled by recycle 2.

## When we get closed
- **With a closed template.** JAK2 with the JAK1 closed model gives a closed structure close to Shan's JAK2 model.
- **And it stays closed once the template is removed.** In JAK2 experiment A all 5 seeds stayed closed with no template
  for recycles 4–15, at the same confidence (pLDDT ~85.7 vs ~86.5 with the template throughout). Closed is a stable state
  for the network, not something the template forces at every step.
- **With no template at a medium MSA depth (128/512)**, in some seeds: the only template-free route to a real closed state
  so far. JAK1 seed 4 is 2.4 Å from the closed reference, at the same pLDDT (~84) as the open seeds.

## Overall
1. **A template decides the state whenever one is used.**
2. **Without a template, MSA depth is the switch:** deep gives open; medium gives both (a bistable regime); shallow gives
   compact, low-confidence structures that are FERM-bound but not the real closed state.
3. **The closed state is genuine for the network:** it can be reached without a template (JAK1, medium MSA) and holds
   without a template (JAK2).
4. **In the bistable regime the outcome isn't reproducible seed by seed.** The meaningful quantity is the fraction of seeds
   that close, and 15 recycles are needed for it to settle.
