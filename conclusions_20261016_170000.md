# JAK3 wild type — MSA depth controls the conformation completely (20261016_170000)

Supersedes [conclusions_20261016_150500.md](conclusions_20261016_150500.md).

## New: JAK3 flips entirely between two MSA depths

| MSA | seeds | final FERM–JH1 (Å) | state | run |
|---|--:|---|---|---|
| **512/8192** (full) | 8 | **94 94 94 94 94 94 94 94** | **all open** | [JAK3_20261016_130116](JAK3/runs/JAK3_20261016_130116/conclusions.md) |
| **128/512** | 16 | 42–56, max 56 | **all compact/closed** | [JAK3_20261016_125830](JAK3/runs/JAK3_20261016_125830/conclusions.md) |

At 512/8192 every seed gives the same answer, open from recycle 1 (mean 88 Å at recycle 1, 94 Å from
recycle 3 on), at higher confidence (pLDDT 82.4–85.9 versus 76.9–84.4 at 128/512). At 128/512 no seed
exceeds 56 Å. There is no overlap between the two depths at all.

This is the cleanest depth-dependence seen in the project. JAK1 behaves similarly (open at deep MSA,
bistable at 128/512); JAK2 differs in closing at 512/8192.

**Consequence for mutation work.** A mutation can only shift a balance that exists. At 512/8192 JAK3 is
already fully open, so there is a ceiling and no opening effect could be seen — the same reason JAK1
V658F showed nothing at deep MSA. 128/512, where wild type is uniformly closed, is the right depth, and
that is where M592F was run.

## M592F result restated in this light

At 128/512, wild type is **closed in 16/16 seeds** (max 56 Å) and M592F reaches **80–97 Å in 5/16**
(p = 0.043). Classification against mapped references identifies the states:

- wild-type compact structure: 13.4 Å from the JAK1 closed model, **16.8 Å from the experimental TYK2
  autoinhibited crystal 4OLI**, 16.2 Å from the AlphaFold JAK3 model;
- M592F extended structure: **3.3 Å from the JAK1 open model**, FERM–JH1 94.2 Å.

So M592F moves JAK3 from the autoinhibited arrangement toward the open one — and the open state it
reaches is the same arrangement wild type adopts spontaneously at full MSA depth (94 Å). Details:
[JAK3/JAK3_M592F.md](JAK3/JAK3_M592F.md).

## Queue revised

The planned second half of 512/8192 (seeds 8–15) was dropped: with 8/8 seeds identical at 94 Å the
depth is saturated and eight more seeds would add nothing. Replaced with **256/1024**, between the two
depths, to locate the transition — the depth where both states appear is the most sensitive one for
mutations, and it is where JAK2 was bistable.

Running now: JAK3 wild type 256/1024 (16 seeds), then 96/384, then the closed-template release;
in parallel JAK3 A573V, M511I, R657Q at 128/512.
