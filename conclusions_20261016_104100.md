Details: [JAK1/JAK1_V658F.md](JAK1/JAK1_V658F.md), [JAK2/JAK2_V617F.md](JAK2/JAK2_V617F.md).

- **JAK1 V658F** has a consistent effect wherever the MSA allows more than one arrangement (128/512, 96/384): it removes the
  closed (autoinhibited-model) arrangement and moves JAK1 into the arrangement of the cryo-EM active dimer 7T6F — which itself
  carries this mutation (mouse V657F). With the whole MSA (open only) or a very shallow MSA it has no effect.
- **JAK2 V617F** changes the JAK2 arrangement strongly at every informative depth, but **the direction depends on MSA depth**:
  closed → open at 512/8192, open → closed/near-closed at 256/1024, detached → open at 128/512, FERM-bound → detached at 96/384.
  No consistent direction — interpret with care (mutant and wild-type MSAs were computed separately).

## Wild type, no template
- **JAK1**: open at deep MSA; bistable (open/closed) only at 128/512; 7T6F-like at 96/384–64/256 ([JAK1/MSA_scan_summary.md](JAK1/MSA_scan_summary.md), [JAK1/JAK1_third.md](JAK1/JAK1_third.md)).
- **JAK2**: closes without template at 512/8192 (7/16, same closed state as with the template, 0.7 Å); all open at 256/1024;
  detached-JH1 intermediate at 128/512 and 64/256 ([JAK2/JAK2_intermediate.md](JAK2/JAK2_intermediate.md)).
