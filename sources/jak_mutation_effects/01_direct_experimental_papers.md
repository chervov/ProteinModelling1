# Direct experimental papers on mutation effects

## Inclusion rule

Included here are primary papers in which a JAK1, JAK2, JAK3, or TYK2 allele (patient-derived, tumour-derived, common population allele, or engineered mechanistic mutant) is tested in at least one functional assay. “Direct” does **not** mean that every result is in a patient-relevant cell type.

## JAK1

| Year | Paper / stable record | Variant(s) or question | Direct evidence and value |
|---:|---|---|---|

| 2019 | [Enzymatic Characterization of Wild-Type and Mutant Janus Kinase 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31683831/) | V658F | Full-length protein enzymology. Crucial caution: constitutive cellular activity was not simply explained by higher intrinsic kinase activity. |

## Critical omissions to resolve before calling this complete

- The table prioritizes studies with explicit functional assays and is deliberately not a complete catalogue of all JAK2 V617F clinical series or every SCID case report.
- JAK2 exon-12 mutation discovery/function studies, JAK1/JAK3/TYK2 somatic cancer sequencing cohorts, and newly indexed 2026 work require export-and-screen from the search protocol.
- Some key articles are cited by their PMID alone here. A formal bibliography should be generated from PubMed/Crossref and deduplicated by PMID/DOI.
