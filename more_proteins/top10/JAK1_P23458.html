dbNSFP indexes only substitutions reachable by a single nucleotide change — about 31% of all amino-acid substitutions are not, and those have no row here. V→F, for instance, needs two changes. A GOF mutation landing in the tolerant cluster is not a failure: every predictor in the panel scores loss of function, so gain-of-function variants are systematically called benign. That is a known, structural limitation of the panel rather than noise.

9.1 Conformations: is there a real open and a real closed state?

What is actually known about this protein occupying more than one inter-domain arrangement.

  • Jeanpierre/Debeaupuis JAK1 open and closed models — ModelArchive ma-a1sai (open, FERM–JH1 92.5 Å) and ma-l9b1f (closed, 40.1 Å) checked this session
    The closed state enters no SWISS-MODEL template lineage, which is why a template-only screen reports JAK1 as rigid.
  • Crystal structure of the JAK2 FERM–SH2 module, PLOS ONE 2016 — the FERM and SH2 domains and their linkers form an integrated module — link checked this session
    Independent support for the rigid FERM–SH2–JH2 block, separate from the cryo-EM rigid-body placement.

9.2 Mutations

Primary reports behind the substitutions computed in section 6.

  • JAK1 V658F is the V617F-equivalent activating position; measured here as a loss of the compact state (0/16 vs 3/5) checked this session

9.3 Drugs and drug resistance

Why the conformational question is a therapeutic question for this protein.

  • JAK1-selective inhibitors (upadacitinib, abrocitinib, itacitinib) are ATP-competitive at JH1, not allosteric at JH2 — unlike the TYK2 agents

9.4 Associated disease

  • JAK1 GOF causes an autoimmune/autoinflammatory syndrome with eosinophilia; JAK1 LOF contributes to immunodeficiency. PTPN2 dephosphorylates JAK1 and JAK3 (not JAK2) checked this session

10. Caveats — read before using any number here

1. Some ΔΔG magnitudes are repulsion, not free energies. The protocol repacks only an 8 Å shell around the mutated site and applies no global FastRelax (that choice buys ~100k variants/hour instead of ~11 minutes per structure). When a larger side chain cannot be accommodated by local repacking alone, the Lennard-Jones repulsive term dominates and the result is an arbitrary large positive number. Any |ΔΔG| above 25 REU is flagged with ⚠ and should be read as “sterically impossible without backbone relaxation”, not as a measured energy. Affected here: A634D.
2. “Open” and “closed” here mean the inter-domain arrangement. The same two words are used in the kinase literature for at least three other things: the G-loop over the ATP site (a few Å), the DFG-in/DFG-out flip (~10 Å), and the WPD loop in phosphatases. A paper reporting that a mutation “stabilises the closed conformation” may well be talking about the active site, not the domain arrangement computed here. See open_close/open_close_inf.md in this repository for the measured comparison of the senses.
3. The predictor panel is blind to gain of function. Every member scores loss of function or loss of stability. A constitutively activating mutation is therefore expected to score benign, and does. Cluster membership is evidence about tolerance, never about direction of effect.
4. Renumbering is sequence-based. Template and experimental coordinates arrive in author numbering; each is mapped to canonical UniProt positions by the offset maximising sequence identity, and the achieved identity is reported in section 1. An offset with low identity means the mapping is unreliable and every per-residue number from that model with it.
5. Coverage limits the geometry. A centroid distance computed from a partial domain is not comparable with one from the complete domain. Pairs where either domain is under 70% present are marked with * in section 2.
