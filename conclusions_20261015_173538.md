## Three kinds of structure
1. **Open** — JH1 away from FERM (FERM–JH1 ~93 Å), matching the open models (AF2, Orianne's ma-a1sai) to ~1–2 Å.
2. **Closed** — JH1 packed on FERM (~40–45 Å) in the arrangement of Orianne's closed model ma-l9b1f (best 1.2 Å for JAK1).
3. **A third arrangement, neither open nor closed — it matches the cryo-EM structure of the active JAK1 dimer**
   (PDB 7T6F, mouse JAK1 V657F, Glassman et al. *Science* 2022): 4.9 Å, same domain distances and the same JH2–JH1
   interface residues ([JAK1/JAK1_third.md](JAK1/JAK1_third.md)). Seen for JAK1 at MSA 64/256 (all 5 seeds): JH1 off FERM
   (FERM–JH1 ~83–87 Å, like open) but in a different place relative to JH2 (47–52 Å from open, ~83 Å from closed);
   also 2 of 5 seeds at MSA 96/384.
   Also "FERM-bound but not closed" structures (JH1 next to FERM in another orientation) appear at shallow MSA.

## Conclusions so far
0. **New: JAK2 reaches the closed state without a template** (512/8192, 3 of 5 seeds; best 6.9 Å from Shan's JAK2), whereas JAK1 needs the narrow 128/512 window.
1. **A template decides the state** (AF2 → open; JAK1 closed → closed).
2. **JAK1 without a template: MSA depth is the switch** — deep → open; 128/512 → both states; ≤ 64/256 → neither
   reference (third arrangement / compact).
3. **The closed state is real for the network:** JAK1 reaches it without any template (1.2 Å from ma-l9b1f), and the
   closed JAK2 stays closed after the template is switched off (A, B).
4. **Most JAK2-specific closed form:** template switched off after recycle 3 (A, B: ~6 Å from Shan's JAK2). More
   templates give higher confidence but not closer to Shan.
5. **Platforms agree:** the same experiment on molab (RTX PRO 6000) and Colab (A100) gives the same result.
6. **Two of our three JAK1 arrangements match experimental structures:** the third arrangement matches the active JAK1
   dimer 7T6F, and the open one matches the other cryo-EM JAK1 dimer 8EWY (3.7–4.5 Å). The closed one matches the
   autoinhibited models (ma-l9b1f, Shan); no experimental full-length autoinhibited JAK structure exists.
