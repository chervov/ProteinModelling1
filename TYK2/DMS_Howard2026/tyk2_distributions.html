TYK2 Variant Score Distributions

Every missense substitution in TYK2 scored sixteen ways by computational predictors and eleven ways by experiment. The shapes below show what each method actually measures — and the matrix shows how little of it is independent.

Computational predictors

Raw score distributions, not sign-corrected. The badge gives the direction that means damaging. Coverage splits at the dbNSFP boundary: six features score every substitution, the rest only those reachable by a single nucleotide change.

Experimental assay arms

All eleven arms. Green is the ~22,500 missense variants; orange is the ~1,187 nonsense (premature-stop) variants, which are non-functional by construction. The distance between the two medians is what the assay has to work with. Separation is that gap divided by the pooled standard deviation — below about 1.5 the arm cannot tell a broken protein from a working one, and those cards are drawn dashed.

missense nonsense (dead protein) solid line = missense median · dashed = nonsense median nonsense bars scaled to their own maximum

Three arms carry no usable signal. At saturating drug (BMS-986202 1 µM and 10 µM, NDI-034858 1 µM) the nonsense median is +0.01 to +0.07 — a protein truncated to nothing scores the same as wild type, because the drug suppresses every variant and scores are expressed relative to wild type. Separation is zero or negative.

IFN-α 1 U/mL is marginal at 0.56: a dead protein reaches only −0.228. Nothing is wrong with the measurement — at limiting ligand there is very little signal to lose, so loss of function has almost nowhere to show.

Why this matters when reading a drug arm. A variant scoring near zero means the drug affects it about as much as it affects wild type — not that its signalling is gone. Without the nonsense control those two readings are indistinguishable.

The seven arms that do separate (2.0–2.7) are the three higher IFN doses, the two low-dose BMS arms, low-dose NDI, and abundance. Note that separation measures only whether an arm can distinguish dead from functional; it says nothing about reproducibility — see the duplicate-arm warning in dms_basic_statistics.md §2.

Eleven of sixteen form one block. MetaRNN and VEST4 reach +0.92 — both are machine-learned meta-predictors trained on the others in the panel.

Three sit outside it. BLOSUM62 (+0.05 to +0.46), GERP (+0.10 to +0.61), and the ΔΔG pair (+0.20 to +0.49). ΔΔG is the only one carrying structural physics rather than sequence.

ΔΔG_diff is null against all fifteen others (−0.00 to +0.03). Its only non-zero entries are with its own two inputs, which are themselves correlated at +0.82.
