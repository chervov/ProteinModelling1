Does cartesian Rosetta fix the per-residue ΔΔG outlier problem?

Direct test on JAK2. The outlier tail reported in DDG_METHODS_SURVEY.md came from the dual-state protocol, which repacks an 8 Å shell (mutate_residue(..., pack_radius=8.0)) and never moves the backbone. cartesian_ddg frees backbone and side-chain degrees of freedom over a 10 Å shell, so it should relieve exactly that strain. Both protocols were run over the same 600 variants — 30 per target residue, all at JH1 positions 855–1118 — so the comparison is matched variant by variant.

Answer in one line. Cartesian fixes the aromatic and branched-aliphatic tail almost completely, reduces proline by 19× but leaves it still an order of magnitude too high, and introduces a new smaller inflation at small and acidic residues. It is nevertheless the better protocol: against ThermoMPNN as an independent reference it reaches Spearman ρ = +0.702 versus the dual-state protocol's +0.529.

1. The table

Both ΔΔG columns are oriented positive = destabilising. The dual-state files natively report ΔΔG = ΔGWT − ΔGmut, i.e. negative = destabilising, so they have been sign-flipped here. Mixing the two conventions in one table is the single most common error in this area and is the reason this is stated twice.

2. What the table says

2.1 Proline: improved 19×, still wrong

Plain Rosetta median 215.1 → cartesian 13.0. The sign and scale are now sane, but ThermoMPNN gives 2.31 and FoldX 4.10 for the same substitutions, so cartesian is still 3–6× too high. Proline's median Δcart_bonded is +3.70, an order of magnitude above every other residue — the residual error is still bond geometry. Freeing a 10 Å shell is not enough: proline removes a backbone amide and constrains φ, which needs genuine backbone rebuilding or fragment insertion. Frenz et al. add exactly that for proline; this reimplementation does not.

2.2 Aromatic and branched aliphatic: fixed

W 7.78 → 2.24, I 7.57 → 3.35, Y 6.23 → 2.62, F 5.73 → 0.98, V 5.40 → 1.55, L 3.67 → 0.13. All now sit within about 1 kcal/mol-equivalent of the ThermoMPNN and FoldX references. This part of the tail was backbone strain, and Cartesian minimisation removes it.

2.3 Small and acidic residues: cartesian makes them worse

D 4.02 → 6.50, G 4.60 → 6.38, E 3.48 → 5.15, S 2.30 → 3.65, N 3.00 → 4.91. Their Δcart_bonded medians are negative, so this is not strain — it is over-relaxation. With the backbone free across a 10 Å shell, replacing a larger residue with a smaller one lets the shell collapse inward and inflates the energy difference. The effect is modest (1–2 units) but it is systematic and in the opposite direction to the proline problem.

Means are useless for plain Rosetta; medians are not. Look at tryptophan: full-scan mean 197.5 against median 7.78. A handful of catastrophic values move the mean by a factor of 25. The same residue under cartesian has mean 5.51 and median 2.24 — still skewed, but by a factor of 2. Never aggregate plain-Rosetta ΔΔG with a mean. This is why the survey's per-residue tables report medians.

4. Practical rules

  1. Prefer cartesian_ddg over repack-only — better agreement with an independent method (ρ 0.702 vs 0.529) and a 27.5% → 18.0% drop in outliers.
  2. Exclude X→P entirely from both protocols. Neither produces a usable proline value.
  3. Treat X→{D, G, E, S, N} with caution under cartesian — mildly inflated by over-relaxation.
  4. Aggregate with medians, never means, and report Δcart_bonded alongside every cartesian value so geometry artefacts are visible.
  5. Flag any variant with |Δcart_bonded| > 2 REU as a geometry artefact rather than a stability result.

5. Method and caveats

600 JAK2 variants, 30 per target residue, sampled with a fixed seed from positions 855–1118 so the 10 Å shell stays inside the JH1 construct (UniProt 849–1124, 276 residues). Structure: AFDB/JAK2_afdb_O60674.pdb from this repository. Scoring: ref2015_cart, PyRosetta 2026.39. Per variant: repack a 6 Å shell, Cartesian-minimise a 10 Å shell with backbone and χ free, iterate to convergence (two lowest within 1 REU, max 5), take the lowest; ΔΔG = min(Emut) − min(Ewt). Runtime 7.2 min for all 600 on 8 cores (0.72 s/variant wall).

Caveat. The construct was relaxed once, not 20× as the Frenz protocol specifies, so absolute cartesian values carry more noise than a production run. Both protocols saw the identical structure and the identical variants, so the comparison itself is sound; the absolute cartesian numbers are indicative rather than final. The plain-Rosetta full-scan columns come from survey/jak2_methods.csv and are production values.

Regenerate with cart_outlier_test.py; per-variant output in cart_vs_dualstate_outliers.csv, per-residue aggregates in per_aa_rosetta_comparison.csv.
