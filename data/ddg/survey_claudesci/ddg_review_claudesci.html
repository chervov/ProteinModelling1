ΔΔG for the 24 selected JAK2 variants — six methods, where each ran, and what to distrust

*Written 6 November 2026. A companion to ../survey/DDG_METHODS_SURVEY.md, not a replacement: that one is proteome-wide over all four JAK kinases; this one is the 24 variants supplied for JAK2, scored by six methods including three the other survey does not have (mCSM-Stability, DDMut, RSALOR) and an independent Rosetta cartesian_ddg run that is a different computation from the dual-state one in data/ddg/.*

Interactive version with figures: ddg_review_claudesci.html. All numbers below were recomputed from saved data for this document; nothing is quoted from memory.

1. Findings, shortest form

  • Only 2 of 23 variants exceed the error bar of the method that produced them. I1065T (+2.32)
and Y317H (+1.68) against ThermoMPNN's measured RMSE of ±1.47 kcal/mol on held-out human data.
  • The six methods agree with each other only moderately — Spearman 0.47–0.70 on the full JAK2
saturation scan. Force-field methods cluster together (FoldX↔ThermoMPNN 0.70), and RSALOR sits apart from them but close to AlphaMissense (0.71).
  • The selected variants are less destabilising than a typical JAK2 substitution — median 33rd
percentile of JAK2's own distribution. For a gain-of-function set this is the expected result: these release JH2 autoinhibition rather than unfold the protein.
  • The proline outlier tail is a Rosetta artefact and it is enormous. For X→P substitutions the
dual-state Rosetta median is −215 REU, against +3.41 for FoldX and +2.03 for ThermoMPNN. 92.5% of X→P fall below −10 in Rosetta, 2.4% above +10 in FoldX, 0.0% in ThermoMPNN. This reproduces finding 3 of the companion survey exactly.
  • rosetta_delta is noise. Spearman |ρ| ≤ 0.02 against every other method, and the two
independent JAK2 runs agree on sign only 55.2% of the time — a coin flip is 50%.
  • My own cartesian_ddg reimplementation has the same disease in miniature. For V625F,
cart_bonded — bond-geometry strain, not stability — contributed +48.67 of +58.55 REU (83%).

8. Suspicious and outlier behaviour

This is the section worth reading twice.

8.1 The proline catastrophe in Rosetta — real, large, and method-specific

X→P: Rosetta median −215.1 REU, FoldX +3.41, ThermoMPNN +2.03. Both other methods call proline substitution mildly destabilising, which is right. Rosetta calls it a two-hundred-unit collapse. Mutating to proline removes a backbone amide and constrains φ; if the protocol does not rebuild the backbone, the result is a geometry violation rather than a thermodynamic quantity.

The aromatic part of the tail is partly shared and therefore partly real. W (46.2% Rosetta / 13.3% FoldX), Y (44.6 / 10.4) and F (43.9 / 6.6) are elevated in both — burying a large aromatic genuinely does cost something. Proline is elevated in Rosetta only. Treat the aromatic tail as signal with an inflated scale, and the proline tail as artefact.

8.1b Does cartesian Rosetta fix the outlier tail? Tested directly — partly.

The tail in §8.1 comes from the dual-state protocol, which repacks an 8 Å shell (mutate_residue(..., pack_radius=8.0)) and never moves the backbone. cartesian_ddg frees backbone and side-chain DOFs over a 10 Å shell, so in principle it should relieve exactly the strain that produces the tail. I ran both protocols over the same 600 JAK2 variants — 30 per target residue, all at JH1 positions 855–1118 so the construct is identical — and compared.

Overall the outlier rate falls from 27.5% to 18.0%, and the answer splits three ways:

  • The aromatic and branched-aliphatic tail is largely fixed. F 37%→3%, L 40%→0%,
Y 43%→17%, W 50%→20%, I 67%→20%, V 40%→13%. Medians come back to physical values — W from −10.3 to +2.2 REU, I from −25.7 to +3.3. This was a backbone-strain artefact and Cartesian minimisation removes it.
  • Proline is not fixed. Still the worst residue by a wide margin: 90% → 60%, median
−253.1 → +13.0 REU. The sign and scale are now sane but the magnitude is still ~6× what FoldX (+3.41) and ThermoMPNN (+2.03) give. Its median Δcart_bonded is +3.7, an order of magnitude above every other residue — so the residual error is still bond geometry. Freeing a 10 Å shell is not enough; proline needs genuine backbone rebuilding or fragment insertion, which is exactly what Frenz et al. add for proline and which this reimplementation omits.
  • Cartesian introduces a new, smaller problem at small and acidic residues.
D 13%→43%, G 13%→37%, E 20%→30%, S 0%→17%, A 0%→10%. Their Δcart_bonded medians are negative, so this is not bond strain — it is over-relaxation: with the backbone free over 10 Å, replacing a large residue with a small one lets the shell collapse inward and the energy difference inflates.

Cartesian is nevertheless the better protocol. Against ThermoMPNN as an independent reference on these 600 variants, cartesian_ddg reaches Spearman ρ = +0.702 against the dual-state protocol's +0.529. The two Rosetta protocols agree with each other only at +0.583 — less than cartesian agrees with a completely different method.

Practical rule: use cartesian_ddg rather than repack-only, exclude X→P entirely, and treat X→{D,G,E,S,A} with caution. Cost on this machine: 0.72 s/variant wall on 8 cores (600 variants in 7.2 min) on a 276-residue construct, after a one-off relax.

8.2 cart_bonded dominates my own cartesian_ddg at packed positions

variantΔΔG (REU)Δcart_bondedfraction
V625F+58.55+48.6783%

Both are flagged and excluded. Cause: MutateResidue places a side chain against a Cartesian-relaxed (hence non-ideal) backbone; if the MoveMap frees backbone DOFs only at i±1 the strain cannot relax. Widening the minimisation shell to 10 Å with backbone and side-chain DOFs free fixed it everywhere except V625F — a Val→Phe at 0% RSA, the most tightly packed site in the set.

What not to do about it: substituting a ramped-repulsion FastRelax makes it worse, because FastRelax is stochastic and relaxes wild type and mutant into different local minima. V617F went from +3.22 to +31.75 REU, at 340 s/variant instead of 4 s. The pack+minimise protocol as reported is deterministic — SD = 0.00 REU over 6 repeats with free random seeds — so its errors are systematic and diagnosable rather than noise. That is a better property than a smaller error.

8.3 rosetta_delta does not reproduce between two runs of the same protein

ddg_JAK2P1_all.json and ddg_JAK2P2_all.json are both complete JAK2 saturation scans (21,508 = 19 × 1,132), so they compare variant by variant:

setnPearson rSpearman ρmedian abs diffsign agreement
all paired rows21,508+0.074+0.1551.3955.4%
after \single-state\< 15 filter15,396+0.295+0.1720.7855.2%

Filtering improves the correlation but not the sign agreement at all. Per-variant delta values are not usable. The mean difference is near zero (−0.005 filtered), so aggregate statistics over thousands of variants may still carry signal.

Unresolved: this directory does not document what P1 and P2 denote. If they are two different open/closed structure pairs, delta is acutely pair-sensitive; if they are repeats of the same pair, it is irreproducible. Both argue against per-variant use but imply different fixes.

8.4 Sign conventions are the most common error in this field, including mine

Three conventions are in play in this repository simultaneously:

sourceconvention
ThermoMPNN, FoldX/ProtVar, my cartesian_ddgpositive = destabilising
mCSM-Stability, DDMutnegative = destabilising (negated on import here)
the dual-state Rosetta files in data/ddg/negative = destabilising (ΔΔG = ΔG_WT − ΔG_mut)

DDG_COMPUTATION_REPORT.md records that scoring with the wrong sign flips AUC against the JAK1 clusters from 0.26 to 0.74, and that the mistake had been made twice. I then made it a third time in the opposite direction, briefly mis-reading the correct negative median of the dual-state files as evidence of contamination; corrected in ../rosetta_dualstate_qc.md. Always fix the convention empirically — check that buried hydrophobic→charged substitutions and helix prolines come out destabilising — rather than trusting documentation.

8.5 Antisymmetry: both learned methods fail it, in the same direction

Thermodynamics requires ΔΔG(A→B) = −ΔΔG(B→A). Measured on Ssym's 341 matched pairs:

methodmean δSDmedian \δ\direct → inverse Pearson
experimental control0.000——r = −1.000 (exact)
ThermoMPNN+0.6890.6670.4970.729 → 0.603
RaSP+1.6261.5071.3140.649 → 0.189 (collapses)

Both predict "destabilising" in both directions. Below ±0.69 kcal/mol the sign of a ThermoMPNN prediction carries no information. RaSP's inverse-direction correlation collapsing to 0.189 is the more serious failure.

8.6 Consensus averaging makes things worse

On 386 held-out human measurements (FireProtDB, homology-filtered, 23 proteins): ThermoMPNN alone r = 0.517 > ThermoMPNN+FoldX 0.509 > all three 0.489. The predictors agree with each other (r 0.583–0.702) more than any of them agrees with experiment (mean 0.424), so averaging reinforces shared bias. A "Cross-Tool Mean" column would be worse than its best member — which is why this survey does not provide one.

8.7 Things that look like outliers but are not

  • ThermoMPNN's range is genuinely narrow, −2.84 to +4.75, with zero values beyond |10|.
Apparent spikes at the edges of the histogram are an artefact of my 0.5/99.5-percentile clipping, not a model ceiling. Checked explicitly before writing this.
  • FoldX's long right tail is real but small: max +69.53, 157 of 20,349 rows above +20 (0.77%).
  • RSALOR's bimodality reflects the RSA scaling, not a bug: buried positions get a weight near 1
and exposed positions near 0, so the distribution piles up at both ends.

9. Known limitations of this survey

  • The RSALOR alignment is shallow. 902 sequences from the UniRef50 clusters of all four JAK
kinases, but Neff = 30.2 — the clusters are nearly all vertebrate orthologues above 80% identity. Adding the three paralogues raised Neff only from 23.9 to 30.2. The EBI HMMER service returns 405 to POST, and a proper jackhmmer/HHblits run needs a ~25 GB database not available here. Treat LOR values as indicative.
  • Rosetta used two constructs, not the full protein. FERM-SH2 (20–497) and JH2-JH1 (545–1132);
full-length 20× relax was not affordable. The other five methods used the full-length AlphaFold model, so Rosetta's structural context differs.
  • FoldX was not run here — those are EBI's precomputed values on AlphaFold models.
  • n = 23 for everything variant-specific. Correlations on that set have wide intervals.
  • No experimental ΔΔG exists for these variants, so none of this is validated against ground
truth for JAK2 specifically. The ±1.47 RMSE is transferred from 386 held-out human measurements on other proteins.

10. Interpretation floors — which threshold answers which question

thresholdvalueuse it to decideflags
experimental noise floor±0.50 kcal/molis the value distinguishable from no effect?16/23 on ≥1 method
ThermoMPNN antisymmetry bias±0.69 kcal/moldoes the sign mean anything?15/23
per-variant random error (RMSE)±1.47 kcal/molis a difference between two variants real?21/23

The accuracy ceiling is not a tooling problem: the theoretical limit from experimental noise is r ≈ 0.70–0.80, and the best independent assessments reach 0.22–0.53 on genuinely unseen mutations. ThermoMPNN's 0.517 here is near the top of that observed band.

ΔΔG is a weak pathogenicity classifier (best reported AUC ≈ 0.66) and is characteristically mild for gain-of-function and dominant-negative variants — which is exactly this set. These numbers describe folding stability only and must not be used to rank pathogenicity.
