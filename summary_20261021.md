# Overnight summary, 2026-10-20 evening to 2026-10-21 morning

## 1. The headline finding: this pipeline measures foldability, not activation

A saturation deep mutational scan of TYK2 was published in eLife on 2026-06-10 (Howard, Abell, …
Dickel; Octant + Bristol-Myers Squibb; bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.10.11.681520). It measures **23,700
substitutions** on two independent readouts, **IFN-alpha signalling** and **protein abundance**, and
the raw scores are on MaveDB (`urn:mavedb:00001270-a-*` signalling, `urn:mavedb:00001271-a-1`
abundance). TYK2 is the **only** JAK with data of this kind - JAK1 has 24 variants, JAK2 and JAK3
none - so this is the one protein in the project where predictions can be checked against
experiment.

Seven substitutions at position 678 were run at 64/256, the only TYK2 depth with dynamic range, and
compared against the measured values.

| variant | our compact | fraction | measured signalling (1 U) | measured abundance |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| F | 0/24 | 0.000 | +0.390 | -0.323 |
| L | 1/16 | 0.062 | +0.145 | -0.183 |
| G | 1/16 | 0.062 | **-0.474** | -0.098 |
| W | 2/16 | 0.125 | +0.227 | -0.361 |
| M | 4/16 | 0.250 | **+0.561** | +0.002 |
| A | 4/16 | 0.250 | -0.228 | -0.139 |
| I | 5/16 | 0.312 | +0.520 | -0.088 |
| WT | 8/24 | 0.333 | - | - |

| axis | Spearman rho | p |
|---|--:|--:|
| **abundance** | **+0.675** | **0.066** |
| signalling | +0.108 | 0.798 |

**Our compact fraction tracks protein abundance and not function.** The cleanest single
demonstration is **G versus M**: they sit at opposite extremes of measured function - G is the
strongest loss-of-function at the position (-0.474), M the strongest gain (+0.561) - and our method
ranks G as the *more* disruptive of the two. No method that responded to function could order them
that way.

This is the same axis the standard predictors occupy. PolyPhen-2, SIFT, REVEL and AlphaMissense all
correlate 2-3x better with abundance than with signalling across ~7,500 TYK2 variants (section 27 of
`mutation_analysis.md`). The conformational pipeline is not reaching an axis those tools miss.

### What this does and does not do to the V678F result

**V678F at 64/256 is 0/24 against wild type's 8/24, p = 0.0003 on the rank test, with both arms at
n = 24.** That measurement stands and survived deepening, unlike most results in this campaign.

What changes is the claim attached to it. **"V678F abolishes TYK2's approaches to the closed
arrangement" is supported. "V678F is an activating mutation and our method detects that" is not** -
the two substitutions that activate TYK2 most strongly in cells, M (+0.561) and I (+0.520), are the
two our method scores closest to wild type.

V678F also remains confounded in a second way: it is one of the two most destabilising substitutions
at the position (abundance -0.323, against a nonsense anchor of -0.352) **and** the only one with a
large literature footprint, being the TYK2 analogue of JAK2 V617F. Those two facts cannot be
separated with the data in hand.

## 3. The n = 8 rule, now quantified

**Seven of eight n = 8 halves in this campaign failed to survive deepening to n = 16.**

The clearest demonstration arrived overnight. V678G and V678A have nearly identical measured
abundance (-0.098 and -0.139) and are the two smallest residues, yet their n = 8 halves landed at
**0/8 and 4/8** - opposite extremes of the entire series. At a true rate near wild type's 0.333 the
binomial standard deviation on eight seeds is **0.166**, so 0/8 is 2 SD low and 4/8 is 1 SD high:
both are ordinary draws from the same distribution. On deepening they converged to 1/16 and 4/16.

**No n = 8 half is interpretable in this assay.** Record it; do not reason from it. This rule was
violated twice during the night by its own author, producing two retractions (sections 31.1 and
31.2 of `mutation_analysis.md`).

## 4. Structural work

- **TYK2 has 52 PDB entities, all X-ray, no cryo-EM and no full-length structure.** 4PO6 covers
  FERM-SH2 (23-583), 4OLI covers JH2-JH1 (566-1187) at 2.8 A, and **no structure contains both FERM
  and JH1**. The FERM-JH1 distance therefore has no experimental value for TYK2 in any state, and
  our 40-45 A "closed" band is **AlphaFold-derived, not crystal-derived**. Only the arrangement
  metric is anchored, by 4OLI.
- **There is no experimental open or extended TYK2 structure.** Published "full-length autoinhibited
  TYK2" models are docked composites of 4PO6 + 4OLI. Everything about an extended TYK2 is
  transferred from JAK1 (Lupardus 2011 EM; Glassman 2022 dimer at FERM-JH1 ~84 A). Our 93 A monomer
  has no counterpart in TYK2 or the family.
- **The 4OLI JH2-JH1 interface is experimentally validated as a stability element.** Its 26 residues
  are 86 % of the way from wild type to dead protein on abundance (p = 5.5e-05), and the effect
  survives matching on solvent accessibility, strongest among partially-exposed residues
  (20-50 % RSA, p = 1.6e-05). With abundance regressed out it also shows a **weak diffuse activating
  signal** (p = 0.00057) but **no hotspot structure** (MAX statistic p = 0.71).
- **V678 is not in that interface.** It buries 0.0 A^2 across the JH2-JH1 contact, sits 14.0 A from
  JH1 and 11-15 A from the 672/674 patch, and is **43.6 % solvent-exposed**. An earlier speculation
  that it levers 672/674 apart was measured and is wrong. It does contact V584 and Q586 at ~4 A,
  which sit beside interface residues 579-582, so a second-shell path exists - stated as a
  hypothesis, not a result.

## 5. Infrastructure

Five distinct faults were found and fixed, all in the recovery path rather than the science:

1. **Detached helpers never wake Claude.** Monitoring launched with `&` runs and writes output but
   sends no completion notification. This silently broke the 30-minute checks for ~2.5 h and left a
   dead sandbox unnoticed for 45 minutes.
2. **Probing a busy kernel kills the running cell.** `execute-code` against a working marimo kernel
   raises `MarimoInterrupt`. Three runs were destroyed this way before the rule became absolute:
   verify from local logs only.
3. **An open molab tab renames the session** and detaches running controllers. `'alive': True` in
   the controller log means the GPU job is still running and the controller will reattach - do not
   relaunch, which discards the in-flight block.
4. **A freshly generated SESSION name is always invalid.** marimo rejects session ids it does not
   know. This existed in *three* places - the controller, the supervisor and the readiness waiter -
   so fixing one let the others overwrite it. All three now leave `SESSION` empty.
5. **`nb_log_age` is not a liveness signal.** During a detach the run log goes stale while the job
   runs. The status check now also prints the controller's `alive` flag.

### Still outstanding

- **Results are pushed only at the end of an 8-seed block.** Five blocks of completed GPU work
  (~7 h) were discarded overnight when sandboxes died before the push. **Per-seed pushing is the
  highest-value remaining change.**
- **A supervisor relaunch replays the queue from the top**, guarded only by a hand-maintained `SKIP`
  set. This produced 12 duplicate run folders across 9 (config, seed-set) groups. The analysis
  deduplicates by seed so no reported number is affected, but the repository is accumulating junk.
  The durable fix is for the controller to check GitHub for already-pushed runs at startup, which
  would also replace the in-memory `PUSHED` set.
- molab sandboxes expire every **1.5-4.5 h**; recovery needs the sandbox URL, which is readable from
  the notebook page's RSC payload without the UI rendering.
