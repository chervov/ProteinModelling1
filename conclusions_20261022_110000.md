# Main results so far (20261022_110000)

Guided-recycling OpenFold (model_1, 15 recycles), MSA depth as the control variable, mutants paired
with wild type on identical seeds. Structures classified by **JH1 RMSD after superposing JH2** (the
arrangement metric) against references mapped onto each target's numbering.

Supersedes `conclusions_20261019_111500.md`. Everything below was recomputed **per seed** on 22 Oct -
860 structures across the four paralogues, the final recycle of every seed of every run - rather than
from the two representative structures each run pushes. Several results changed; the ones that
changed are flagged. Detail and derivations are in `mutation_analysis.md` sections 35-49.

---

## 0. Three corrections to the reference set, made before anything else

These invalidate parts of earlier documents and should be read first.

- **7T6F and 8EWY are mouse JAK1**, not JAK2 (98.0 % identity to JAK1_MOUSE against 43.1 % to JAK2).
  Both are the same construct in two arrangements. Consequence: **there is no experimental
  multi-domain structure for TYK2, JAK2 or JAK3 at all.** Every closed-state number for those three is
  a comparison against a prediction or against a cross-paralogue JAK1 structure.
- **AlphaFold does not predict a compact JAK.** Measured FERM-JH1: TYK2 39.7 A and JAK3 41.5 A
  (compact), but **JAK1 92.3 A and JAK2 93.8 A (extended)**. "The AF2 model is compact" holds for half
  the family. AlphaFold reports FERM-JH1 PAE of 18.5-25.8 A for all four - it is not confident about
  any of them, and does not give the same answer twice across paralogues.
- **4OLI contains no FERM domain.** It is the TYK2 JH2-JH1 crystal. The figure "closed state has
  FERM-JH1 of 40-45 A" never came from a crystal structure; it comes from AF2 TYK2 and Jeanpierre's
  closed JAK1 model, both predictions. Matching 4OLI constrains only how the kinase sits on the
  pseudokinase.

A validation in the other direction: the two readouts are formally independent - the arrangement
metric never looks at FERM - yet across all 860 structures they agree in **488 of 488** cases where
both are defined. And the FERM-JH1 calculation reproduces the repository's own stored `ferm_jh1`
column to a median of 0.002 A.

## 1. MSA depth decides the conformation, and TYK2's ladder is now complete

| TYK2 WT, template-free | 32/64 | 64/256 | 96/384 | 128/512 | 256/1024 | 512/8192 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| reaches canonical open | 0/15 | 2/24 | 7/8 | 8/8 | **9/9** | **9/9** |
| median FERM-JH1 (A) | 45.1 | 90.5 | 93.6 | 93.5 | 93.2 | 93.3 |
| median pLDDT | 76.8 | 80.1 | 80.7 | 81.7 | 82.2 | 82.5 |

A **step** between 64/256 and 96/384, then saturation - the two deep blocks run overnight (256/1024
and 512/8192, 8 seeds each) are 100 % open and add nothing beyond confirming there is no further
transition. The prediction stated in advance for 256/1024, "open near 93 A", is confirmed.

**Changed from 19 Oct:** that document reported TYK2 as having "no depth where both states appear".
With per-seed scoring, 64/256 does show both - but only 2/24 open, and the rest are *not* closed
(see 2).

## 2. The closed state is template-induced, not predicted

**2 of 276 template-free TYK2 structures** land within 15 A of a closed reference, against **57 of 57**
when 4OLI is supplied. The same holds family-wide: JAK1 5/129 and TYK2 2/276 reach the compact
arrangement from sequence, against JAK2 42/237 and JAK3 34/136.

**The "compact < 60 A" bin contains no closed structures.** Of 14 free-run TYK2 structures below 60 A,
zero are the closed arrangement. The figure "WT 7/16 compact at 64/256", which made 64/256 the
campaign's informative depth, was never counting closed structures.

**Changed from 19 Oct:** "V678F abolishes wild type's partial approaches to the closed arrangement"
cannot be stated - those approaches were not to the closed arrangement.

## 3. The variant effect, measured at full power

TYK2 at 64/256, template-free, fraction of seeds reaching the **canonical open** arrangement (within
15 A of cryo-EM 8EWY or Jeanpierre's open model), Holm-corrected over nine comparisons:

| variant | open / n | fraction | Holm |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| WT | 2/24 | 0.083 | - |
| **V678F** | 18/24 | **0.750** | **<0.0001** |
| **V678A** | 10/16 | **0.625** | **0.0033** |
| **V678I** | 8/16 | **0.500** | **0.0479** (marginal) |
| V678D | 7/16 | 0.438 | 0.105 |
| V678G | 6/16 | 0.375 | 0.210 |
| P982A | 5/16 | 0.312 | 0.377 |
| V678M | 4/16 | 0.250 | 0.586 |
| V678L | 3/24 | 0.125 | 1.000 |
| V678W | 1/16 | 0.062 | 1.000 |

Robust to the threshold: V678F separates from wild type at **every cut from 8 to 30 A**
(p = 7e-4 to 5e-6). This is the project's first well-powered, corrected, experimentally-referenced
variant effect.

**It does not correlate with the deep mutational scan.** Over the nine V678 points, the
arrangement-based score gives |rho| < 0.09 against abundance and against signalling at both doses.
The *compact fraction* - a different score on the same structures - gives rho = +0.56 against
abundance and **+0.67 against signalling at 100 U/mL (p = 0.050)**, which earlier work never tested.
Which dose the scan is read at decides whether the pipeline looks like an abundance sensor or an
activation sensor. One uncorrected test out of six: a hypothesis, not a result.

## 4. The template-release assay is dead

| variant | n | closed | median FERM-JH1 | median RMSD to closed ref |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| WT | 8 | 8 | 38.9 | 1.5 |
| **H582W** | 8 | **8** | **39.2** | **1.5** |
| P982A | 8 | 8 | 39.3 | 1.6 |
| V678F | 13 | 13 | 39.1 | 1.6 |
| V678M | 8 | 8 | 38.7 | 1.6 |
| V678W | 8 | 8 | 38.9 | 1.6 |
| V678I | 4 | 4 | 39.3 | 1.5 |

**57/57.** H582W was the decisive test - the most activating substitution at any 4OLI interface
residue (IFN-alpha +1.339, 99.9th percentile of 23,723 variants) with abundance intact, and the queue
text said in advance that if it did not release, the assay cannot detect destabilisation at all. It
is indistinguishable from wild type.

Seven sequences spanning the full measured range of both DMS axes give the same answer with a total
spread of **0.6 A**. The assay has no dynamic range; it reports the template, not the protein.
**This retracts the earlier negative claim** that no V678 substitution destabilises the closed state -
the assay cannot support a negative result because it cannot support a positive one. The
template-free results in section 3 are unaffected.

## 5. Paralogue differences

| | n | compact | open model/dimer | active dimer | unmatched |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| JAK1 | 129 | 5 | 55 | **35** | 34 |
| JAK2 | 237 | 42 | 96 | 0 | 99 |
| JAK3 | 136 | 34 | 28 | 0 | 74 |
| TYK2 | 276 | 2 | 109 | 2 | 163 |

- **The active-dimer (7T6F) arrangement is JAK1-only** - 35/129, against 0, 0 and 2. JAK1 V658F at
  96/384 is 16/16. But 7T6F *is* mouse JAK1, so this is a paralogue reproducing its own experimental
  reference: a positive control that the pipeline can hit a measured arrangement, not evidence that
  JAK1 is special.
- **JAK2 never reaches an experimentally characterised extended state** (0/237) although 96 of its
  structures match extended AF2 JAK2.
- **JAK3's extended state matches cryo-EM open JAK1 at 4.9-7.6 A** and Jeanpierre's open model at
  1.6-3.5 A. Its compact bin splits into three populations, not one.
- Both TYK2 and JAK3 reproduce the experimental open JAK1 arrangement to 5-8 A from sequence alone at
  deep MSA, with no template. That is the only thing in this project validated against a measured
  multi-domain arrangement.

## 6. A TYK2-specific arrangement that is not in the reference set

40 % of the family (370/860) matches nothing within 15 A. That class is **not noise**. Clustering the
118 unassigned TYK2 structures at 64/256 against each other gives 16 clusters with only 5 singletons.

The dominant one: **n = 54 (46 %), FERM-JH1 94.6 A, members a median 7.5 A from each other but 22.7 A
from the nearest reference** - three times closer to one another than to anything known. Present in
all ten variants across 20 independent runs.

It is **not a pipeline artefact**: scored against its own medoid, 54/118 TYK2 structures fall within
15 A, against **1 of 207** from the other three paralogues (JAK2 0/99, closest 60.2 A; JAK3 0/74,
closest 41.8 A). Fisher p = 3.8e-27.

Not established that it means anything. It is a prediction at the one depth where TYK2 reaches
neither state cleanly, with pLDDT indistinguishable from everything else, and TYK2 has no experimental
structure to check against. It is the first structural feature specific to TYK2 rather than shared
with the family - a reason to examine it, not to believe it.

## 7. Three of four readouts do not measure what they were used for

- **pLDDT is blind to arrangement.** Median 80.2 for closed, 80.4 for canonical open, 80.1 for
  structures more than 15 A from everything; interquartile width 0.7 points across 323 structures
  spanning 39-95 A. Within each MSA depth the correlation with distance-to-nearest-reference is absent
  and the signs disagree (+0.02, +0.14, -0.28, +0.06); the pooled rho = -0.47 is Simpson's paradox
  with depth driving both. **The objective J = (1 - pLDDT_norm) + 0.1 x violation_loss selects among
  seeds at fixed depth - exactly where pLDDT is uninformative.** The method is better described as
  "MSA subsampling plus external-reference classification" than as guided recycling.
- **Centroid distances cannot separate variants.** WT vs V678F: FERM-JH1 p = 0.012, JH2-JH1 p = 0.001,
  superposition p = 4.6e-06. V678W and V678F have nearly the same FERM-JH1 (93.6 vs 94.6) and opposite
  open fractions (0.06 vs 0.75) - distance says nothing about orientation.
- **The release assay returns a constant** (section 4).
- **What survives: MSA depth as the control variable, superposition against an experimental reference
  as the readout.** That combination produced every significant result above.

Also: `openness_A` in `index.csv` is the **JH2-JH1** distance, not FERM-JH1 (46.585 against a run's
own RESULT payload reporting JH2-JH1 46.582, FERM-JH1 93.1). Since every threshold quoted in this
project is a FERM-JH1 figure, reading that column as "openness" would make every TYK2 run look closed.

## 8. Next experiment

**Delete FERM from the input.** Three early JAK1 runs accidentally used a 586-residue JH2-JH1
construct. At 32/64 they hit the 4OLI arrangement at **7.3 A and 17.5 A with pLDDT ~88**; full-length
JAK1 at the same depth is never closer than **36.9 A, pLDDT 70-79**. 4OLI is itself a JH2-JH1 crystal,
so this is the positive control the project never had.

n = 2 by accident. One 8-seed run each for TYK2 and JAK1 would settle whether **FERM placement is what
actually fails** - and if so, every closed-state result here is a statement about FERM, not about the
kinase-pseudokinase interface, and the interface work stands on firmer ground than the arrangement
work. No new code, one config change.

Secondary: a second position for the variant series (all nine well-sampled substitutions are at 678),
and varying the number of imposed template recycles (1, 2, 3, 6) to find where the 4OLI basin stops
capturing the model - at 3 it captures everything.
